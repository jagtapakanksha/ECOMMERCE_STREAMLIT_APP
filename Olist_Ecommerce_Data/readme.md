Olist Brazilian E-Commerce Dataset
The Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist is a real-world, anonymized dataset containing approximately 100,000 orders placed between 2016 and 2018 across Brazilian marketplaces. It provides a comprehensive view of the e-commerce lifecycle, from customer purchases and payments to shipping, delivery, and customer reviews. 

Key data areas:
- Orders: Order status, purchase timestamps, and delivery dates.
- Customers and Sellers: Customer and seller locations across Brazil.
- Products and Sales: Product categories, item prices, and freight costs.
- Payments: Payment methods, installments, and transaction values.
- Reviews: Customer ratings and written feedback.
- Geolocation: Brazilian ZIP-code prefixes and geographic coordinates. 

How we use it in our E-commerce Intelligence application:
We load the dataset into Snowflake, organize it into RAW and STAGING layers, and use it to build interactive Streamlit dashboards and explore business questions such as revenue trends, product performance, delivery delays, and customer satisfaction. It also provides a practical foundation for demonstrating AI agents that answer business questions using data.

Source: https://www.kaggle.com/datasets/olistbr/brazilian-ecommerce
